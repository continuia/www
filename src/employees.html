Flexible pricing

Three ways to bring Continuia into your benefits program

Affinity model

Member Discount

No upfront cost to the employer. Employees get a discount on every case. Good for low-budget employers or a pilot program.

Predictable pricing

PEPM Subscription

$1-2 per employee per month for full plan-wide access, priority support, and usage analytics. Good for employers who want predictable budgeting.

Shared cost model

Common questions

What HR teams usually ask

Q

Do employees see pricing before committing?

Yes. Whatever model you choose, employees see the exact cost, if any, before starting a case. No hidden fees.

Q

Will an employee's own doctor be notified?

No, not unless the employee chooses to share the results. Every review is confidential and HIPAA-aligned.

Q

How fast does an employee get their opinion?

Most employees receive a written second opinion within 48-72 hours of their case and records being complete.
